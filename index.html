AFor Carola

Kanda Myojin, the shrine

Akihabara's own shrine, about 10 minutes on foot from the café. We can show how to pray, how to draw a fortune, and the wooden plaques where people write their wishes. Many of them are drawn in anime style.

  • Kanda Myojin on Google Maps
  • Official site

BFor Alberto

G-Store Akiba, Jump shelves

Anime clothing on the 4th floor of the Onoden building. Half the shop is Jump titles, so One Piece, Hunter×Hunter and Dragon Ball are all here. The entrance is easy to miss, so Yuuki leads.

  • G-Store Akiba on Google Maps

CFor Alberto

Trader, figures

The main store of Trader is big and bright, with a lot of prize figures. A good place to look for One Piece and Dragon Ball.

  • Trader on Google Maps

DFor both

GiGO, retro games

The 6th floor is a retro arcade, with machines you can play together: Street Fighter II, Pac-Man, pinball. There are seats if anyone wants to rest.

  • GiGO retro games on Google Maps

EFor both

Maidreamin, the maid café

The maid café you both asked for. Heaven's Gate is right by the station, and there is a good chance of a live show. You choose what to order there. It is a good last stop, because the station is one minute away.

How today was made, behind the scenes

Alberto, you are studying AI here, so this is the inside view. Between your booking this morning and the moment we meet, a team of AI assistants did the preparation. Yuuki made the decisions.

  • 08:19You booked. Your note said: a 25-year-old anime fan, a mother who loves culture, and a maid café for both of you.
  • 10:30An assistant wrote your welcome email. It read your note, confirmed that we meet inside Vie de France, and sent you the short questionnaire. New bookings are spotted within 15 minutes, with nobody watching.
  • 10:59You answered: One Piece, Hunter×Hunter, Dragon Ball, retro games, and a maid café.
  • 12:30The places on this page were chosen from your answers. Most came from Yuuki's own notes on Akihabara. The shrine was not in those notes, so it was checked against its official website first.
  • 15:00Yuuki meets you. From here it is a person, not a screen.

Yuuki runs his work with 13 AI assistants. Each has one job and its own workspace, so they do not step on each other. One model gathers information, a second one checks it, and only then does it go into a plan.

The assistants do the research, the drafts and the checking. A person decides. If you want to ask how any of it works, ask on the way. Alberto, that goes double for you.
